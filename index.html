Privacy Policy

Fuel Price Tracker · Last updated: 12 November 2026

This policy explains how the Fuel Price Tracker app for Android (“the app”) handles information. The app is made by Ganidu Madushan Balasuriya (“I”, “me”, “the developer”).

In short

  • The app does not ask you to create an account.
  • It does not collect your name, email address, phone number, location, contacts or any other personal information.
  • It has no advertising. It sends anonymous crash reports and usage statistics, which you can turn off in Settings.
  • The settings you choose stay on your device.

Information stored on your device

The app saves the following on your device only, so that it works quickly and offline:

  • your appearance (light or dark), language and notification choices;
  • whether you have already seen the welcome screen;
  • the price alert limits you set, if any;
  • the most recent fuel prices and Brent crude data it downloaded, which are also used by the home screen widget.

This information is not sent to me or to any server by the app. If you have Android backup turned on, your phone may include the app’s data in its normal device backup, which is managed by Google under your own account settings.

Information the app connects to

To show current prices, the app downloads fuel prices and Brent crude oil data from Google Cloud Firestore, a service provided by Google. Like any internet request, this reveals your device’s IP address and basic connection details to Google, which handles them under its own privacy policy. I do not collect or store IP addresses, and I do not link these requests to you or build a profile about you.

App verification (App Check)

To make sure prices are only served to the genuine app and not to bots or copies that have been tampered with, the app uses Firebase App Check with Google Play Integrity. When the app asks for prices, Google’s Play Integrity service checks that the request comes from an unmodified copy of the app on a genuine Android device, and the app receives a short-lived token that it sends along with the request. Google performs this check and receives limited device and app information for it, which it handles under its own privacy policy. I do not receive or store this information. I only see anonymous totals of verified and unverified requests.

Price alerts and notifications

If you set a price alert, the app checks fuel prices in the background, including when the app is closed, and sends you a notification when a price goes above or below a limit you set. The limits stay on your device. Alerts are checked against CEYPETCO prices.

Price change notifications use Firebase Cloud Messaging, a service provided by Google. The app subscribes your phone to a general “price updates” topic for your chosen language, and Google delivers a message to everyone on that topic when CEYPETCO prices change. To make this work, Google assigns your phone an anonymous app installation identifier, which it handles under its own privacy policy. I do not receive or store this identifier, I cannot tell which phones are subscribed, and I cannot send anything to you individually. You can turn price change notifications off in the app’s Settings, which also removes the subscription.

Notifications require your permission, which you can turn off at any time in your phone’s settings. The app also asks to restart price alert checks after your phone reboots.

Crash reports and usage statistics

To find and fix problems and to see which features are used, the app uses Firebase Crashlytics and Firebase Analytics, services provided by Google.

  • Crash reports: if the app crashes, a report is sent containing technical details: your device model, Android version, app version, and what the app was doing when it stopped.
  • Usage statistics: anonymous events such as which screens are opened, that a price picture was shared, or which language or appearance was chosen. They contain no text you typed.

Both use a random app installation identifier that is not linked to your name, email address, location or any other personal information. The app does not use the Android advertising ID and does not use this data for advertising. Google handles the data under its own privacy policy and Firebase privacy information. You can turn both off at any time in the app under Settings > Privacy > Crash reports & usage statistics.

Sharing

If you tap Share, the app puts the current prices into your phone’s standard share sheet and you choose where they go. The app does not send them anywhere itself and does not see which app or person you pick.

QR Pass

The QR Pass page lets you keep your National Fuel Pass QR code on your phone. After you scan it, the code is encrypted with a key held in the Android Keystore and stored only on this device. It is excluded from cloud backups and device transfers, shown only after you confirm with your fingerprint, face or screen lock, and locked again when you leave the app or the page. The code is never sent to any server and is not included in crash reports or usage statistics. You can delete it at any time from the QR Pass page, and uninstalling the app removes it.

Third-party services

  • Google Firebase / Cloud Firestore supplies the price data the app downloads. See Google’s privacy policy and Firebase privacy information.
  • Firebase Cloud Messaging delivers price change notifications (see “Price alerts and notifications” above).
  • Firebase App Check and Google Play Integrity check that requests come from the genuine app (see “App verification” above).
  • Firebase Crashlytics and Firebase Analytics provide crash reports and anonymous usage statistics (see “Crash reports and usage statistics” above).

The app does not include advertising networks or any other third-party tracking tools.

Links to other websites

Some links in the app open in your browser, for example to CEYPETCO, the U.S. Energy Information Administration or the developer’s website. These sites have their own privacy policies, and I am not responsible for how they handle information.

Children

The app is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Your choices

  • Delete your price alerts in the app at any time.
  • Turn off crash reports and usage statistics in the app’s Settings.
  • Turn off price change notifications in the app’s Settings, or turn off notifications for the app in your phone’s settings.
  • Clear the app’s data in your phone’s settings, or uninstall the app, to remove everything it has stored on your device.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page and in the app, with an updated date at the top.
